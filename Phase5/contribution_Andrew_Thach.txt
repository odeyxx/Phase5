Andrew Thach - Phase 5 Contribution

I worked on experiments, plots, the README and reproducibility documentation,
and demo support.

Specifically, I built:

- The loss injection helper that drops or corrupts segments at a configured
  probability, used in Options 1, 4, 5 and the Chart 1 sweep.
- The experiments.py script that runs the Chart 1 sweep across loss rates
  0% to 95% in 5% steps, averaging multiple runs per rate.
- The make_plots.py script that produces all three required charts:
    - Chart 1 (completion time vs packet loss rate)
    - Chart 2 (cwnd vs transmission round)
    - Chart 3 (Phase 1 to Phase 5 completion-time comparison)
- Per-run wall-clock cap so high-loss runs cannot hang the experiment script.
- The README with run instructions for each option, debugging tips
  (--verbose, --seed), and reproducibility steps for the charts.
- The demo script that walks through all five required scenarios in order.
- Validation runs across all five options to confirm byte-for-byte correctness
  of the received output.
- Demo video preparation and recording support.
