Kevin Pol - Phase 5 Contribution

I worked on the receiver side of the TCP-like protocol, the flow control logic,
and in-order reassembly.

Specifically, I built:

- The receiver's three-way handshake response (receive SYN, send SYN-ACK with
  initial rwnd, accept final ACK).
- Checksum validation and segment parsing on the receive path.
- Cumulative ACK generation that advertises the current rwnd in every ACK.
- The receiver buffer model that tracks free space and supports flow control.
- The Option 2 demo configuration where rwnd is capped at 2 segments and the
  application drains slowly to show the sender pausing and resuming.
- In-order reassembly with out-of-order buffering, so a single fast retransmit
  can fill the gap without forcing a Go-Back-N style cascade of retransmissions.
- FIN handling and the final ACK that closes the connection.
- The output file write that proves byte-for-byte correctness against the input.

I also helped fix the bug where the receiver was sending two ACKs per accepted
DATA segment, which was being misread by the sender as duplicate ACKs.
